FAQ

Common questions

How much does a home elevator cost?

It depends on the number of floors, the space available and the finish you choose. After a free site visit, we give you an exact quotation.

Can a lift be installed in my existing house?

In many cases, yes. During the site visit, our team checks the space and tells you the best option for your home.

How many floors can the elevator serve?

We build lifts for 2 floors (G+1), 3 floors (G+2) and taller homes. Tell us how many floors you have and we'll design for it.

Is it safe and easy for elderly people to use?

Yes. Our lifts are designed for seniors and people with knee problems or illness, with simple controls and a smooth ride.

What does the warranty cover?

Every NaikDown lift has a 2-year warranty from installation, and our after-sales team continues to provide service and maintenance after that.
